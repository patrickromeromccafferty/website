
## An August Work Party

<br><br> <br><br> 

An August Work Party is the debut album by anoraq, a Glasgow-based spoken word band. <br><br> 









Patrick Romero McCafferty (vocals) • Tim Martin Ciubotaru (guitar) • Alex Palmer (percussion) • Lewis Hall (keys) • Peter Alec Kay (fiddle & bass). 

<br><br> 

Anoraq have supported as diverse acts as LYR (Simon Armitage) and hip-hop duo Frankie Stew & Harvey Gun. [Inside Voices](insidevoices.md), a poetry and music night at King Tut’s, Scotland’s leading small music venue.
