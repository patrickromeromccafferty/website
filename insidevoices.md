## Inside Voices

[**Inside Voices**](https://www.instagram.com/insidevoices____/) is an music and poetry night that runs monthly at King Tut's, Glasgow. Since 2023, it has showcased many of Scotland's emerging poets, with special events including closing Paisley Book Fest and the Schpiegel Tent at Connect Festival. 

In 2025 the night ran in collaboration with a number of literary organisations shaping Scottish literature: SPAM Zine, Glasgow Review of Books, UBelong Glasgow, The Morning Star, Anam Creative, and crisp packet poetry.
